How I work

I start from the data model, not the screen. Most of what I build is multi-tenant, background-job heavy and integration-first — so isolation, idempotency and observability get designed in on day one rather than patched in later.

Then I take it all the way to the browser: typed API contracts, a React or Django-templated front end that matches, and deploys that don't need me awake.
